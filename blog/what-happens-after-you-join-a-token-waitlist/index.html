What Happens After You Join a Token Waitlist

Joining a waitlist is a small act. You enter your number, you confirm, and

you are in line. But most waitlists go quiet after that first tap, and the

silence is where interest dies. So it is worth saying plainly what happens

next on the Embodier waitlist, step by step, so you know exactly where you

stand from the first minute.

You are on the list. Nothing else is required.

The moment your number is confirmed, your place is held. There is no

application to fill out yet, no documents to gather, no calls to take. The

waitlist is exactly what it sounds like: a list, in order, of people who

asked to be kept close while the program takes shape. You will not be

asked for anything you are not ready to give.

A text message confirms your place

Everything after signup arrives by text message. A short confirmation

tells you your place is held. From time to time after that, you will

receive brief updates: what the team is working on, which properties are

being studied, and when the next step opens for people on the list. The

messages are occasional and short. If you ever want them to stop, one

reply ends them.

This is deliberate. The phone is where the whole program lives. Signup,

updates, and eventually the application itself all happen through the

number you joined with. There are no passwords to remember and no accounts

to manage. Your number is your identity here, confirmed by a short code

sent to your phone.

The refundable reservation

At any point, you may choose to place a fully refundable $100 deposit to

reserve your place. This is the program's good-faith handshake. It moves

you from interested observer to someone with a reserved place in line,

and it is credited toward your participation later.

The word refundable does all the work in that sentence. The deposit is

returned in full, at any time, for any reason, no questions asked. There

is no window that closes, no fine print that narrows it. If you change

your mind next week or next year, the full amount comes back to you.

The only exception is a commitment to fund that is not honored, in which

case the deposit is kept as liquidated damages — and even then, the

amount kept is exactly the deposit, nothing more.

Payment is one tap. The standard path is Apple Pay, the way you already

pay for everything else on your phone. Larger commitments can use manual

card entry or wire instructions, but the reservation itself is always the

same single tap.

Education before anything else

Before any application opens, the waitlist is an education. Tokenized

ownership of real resorts is a new idea for most people, and new ideas

deserve plain explanations. Expect short pieces on how tokenization

works, how custody is handled by a licensed third party rather than by

Embodier itself, and what it means to hold a stake in a place rather

than a night in one.

None of this is advice, and none of it asks you to decide anything. It

is context, delivered in small pieces, so that when the time comes to

consider an application, you are deciding from understanding rather than

from momentum.

The whitelist and the application

When the program is ready for applications, the waitlist becomes the

whitelist. That word matters: it means people on the list are considered

before anyone else, in order. The application itself is straightforward —

identity verification, eligibility checks, and your stated interest. There

is no bidding, no rush, no pressure. The list exists precisely so that

nobody has to hurry.

What does not happen yet

It is equally important to say what the waitlist is not. Joining the

list is not a purchase. The refundable reservation is not a token, not a

share, and not an investment. No tokens are issued, traded, or transferred

at this stage. Exchange trading and any secondary market activity remain

gated behind licensing. Unlocks with our pending El Salvador license

approval.

This is not a limitation to work around. It is the structure that makes

the program credible: everything that can operate today — onboarding,

education, the waitlist, refundable reservations — operates today, and

everything that requires a license waits for the license.

When your reservation converts

If and when you decide to participate, your reservation converts

smoothly. The deposit you placed is credited toward your participation,

and your place in line — held since the day you joined — carries forward.

People who joined early are considered early. That is the entire reward

for being early: order, clarity, and a team that knows your name.

If you decide not to participate, the deposit returns to you in full and

the list keeps your place only as long as you want it. Leaving is as

simple as joining was.

to visit could be places you partly own, held for decades, run

beautifully, never flipped. It starts with a number and a text message.

Everything after that is up to you.
