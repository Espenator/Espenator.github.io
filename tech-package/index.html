Questions, answered

Is this a live token sale?
No. We are pre-license: the platform is in development pending licensing in El Salvador. Today we collect waitlist signups and soft commitments only. No tokens are offered for sale and no secondary trading exists.
What does the $100 reservation do?
It holds your place in line when bidding opens on a project. It is fully refundable at any time for any reason, and credited toward the $500 token minimum at the offering. It is not a token purchase and creates no investment entitlement.
When do tokens actually launch?
After our El Salvador license is granted and offering documents, identity verification, and signed subscriptions are complete. Timelines are estimates — the waitlist keeps you first in line.
Who holds my tokens and money?
Embodier.ai is the marketing and sales partner — it never holds client assets. Tokens are held by a licensed third-party custodian you designate, and committed funds sit in independent escrow.
Are returns guaranteed?
No. Nothing in the program guarantees returns, distributions, or appreciation. Digital assets are high-risk and illiquid — you may lose your entire investment. Nothing here is investment advice.
