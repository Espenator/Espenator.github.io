## Organic

- **Heavy:** IG Reels + TikTok EN/ES, daily-ish rotation of 4 cuts; Stories GIF + reply CTA.
- **Medium:** X @Embodierai + Postiz.
- **Light:** LinkedIn (not desire heroes — capital/process only).
- **Skip TOF:** email capture, FB static-only, Pinterest, cold LI InMail for retail tokens.
- **Pillars:** desire hook; exclusivity; lifestyle B-roll + CTA; soft proof only.

## Paid

- **Priority:** Meta core; TikTok LATAM CTWA; YT/X retarget light; LinkedIn capital separate.
- **Phase 1 Learn:** broad geo US+LATAM; split by spokesperson×lang; ThruPlay + CTWA/SMS.
- **Phase 2 Convert:** retarget 50%+/75% viewers → waitlist.
- **Phase 3 Scale:** LAL from confirmed waitlist; keep gender lock.
- **KPIs:** hook rate, hold, CTR, cost/WA-SMS start, cost/OTP waitlist.
- **Budget shape:** ~60% Meta / 25% TikTok LATAM / 10% retarget / 5% tests (relative, not $).
